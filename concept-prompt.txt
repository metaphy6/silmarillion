Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one wide landscape visual-development plate for an isometric narrative strategy game report.
Primary request: A vast isometric woodland frontier from Tolkien's First Age, a proposed small crossing in Brethil near the River Teiglin. Beautiful SMALL people, tents, carts and everyday objects are tiny relative to the immense continuous landscape. Painterly atmosphere inspired by Disco Elysium's expressive environments, melancholy yet intensely alive.
Scene: a winding slate-blue river cuts diagonally across a very large moss-green and birch woodland landscape. A shallow stony ford connects an ancient dirt road on two banks. A modest refugee camp under trees with a few amber lanterns and muted cloth shelters. Tiny watchwardens, a grey-cloaked Elven envoy, travelling families with packs, a few loaded wooden carts. Old weathered roots, reeds, granite shelves and tracks. Several pathways with alternative routes create legible strategic geography. Distant edges fade into rain and woodland mist, but there is no horizon or sky band.
Composition: fixed orthographic isometric camera, high oblique overhead angle, broad zoomed-out game-world view, landscape continues beyond all four edges, no floating diorama platform. Terrain and negative space dominate 90 percent of the image. 12 to 18 tiny humanoid figures distributed in two small groups, each only 1.5 to 2 percent of image height, NEVER a large foreground hero. Carts and tents appropriately small. No buildings taller than a few trees. Human-made clearing occupies less than 8 percent of visible world. Careful edge and value separation keeps small groups readable.
Style: mature expressive oil and gouache paint, visible broken brushstrokes, atmospheric washed edges, textured forest floor, dark viridian and blue shadow, warm amber and faded vermilion accents, silver birch highlights. Environmental storytelling, fragile warmth inside an enormous indifferent yet beautiful world, not uniformly muddy or brown.
Constraints: no text, no labels, no HUD, no logos, no portraits, no large characters, no photorealism, no cartoon mobile game, no glossy plastic, no oversized weapons. This is an original proposed local composition, not a reproduction of a book illustration or a canonical cartographic map.
