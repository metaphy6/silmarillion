# Peoples production roster, revision 3

Every faction creates or recreates **exactly one living hero**, with a unique faction-specific type. Appearance, history and build can vary within that type. Ordinary officers and companies do not become additional heroes. Hero recreation restores the selected identity; it does not permit a duplicate.

All abilities and production designs below are original proposals. These two signature outputs supplement each faction's ordinary food, housing, storage, transport and repair. Production consumes materials, workers, facilities and time; hero attention enhances specialist work without being required for every routine job.

## Eight Elven selection profiles

These are eight playable profiles, not eight equally ranked ancestral clans. Three houses share Noldorin ancestry; Falmari, Sindar and Nandor share Telerin ancestry. Avari supplies an authored local clan. Heritage determines specialist knowledge, not compulsory allegiance or moral conduct.

| Profile and ancestry | One unique hero type | Signature production 1 | Signature production 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vanyar**, Eldarin kindred | **Concord Singer:** prepares collective action through song and agreement. | **Chorus Court, building:** stone, instruments and skilled labor enable coordinated company support. | **Banner Fellowship, unit:** trained, equipped guards protect neighboring formations. |
| **House of Fëanor**, Noldorin dynasty | **Jewelwright:** develops exceptional items with consequential design tradeoffs. | **Gem Atelier, building:** gems, metals and specialist tools support limited masterwork queues. | **Bound-light Jewel, magic item:** prolonged craftsmanship creates portable illumination or signaling; choose its function when commissioned. |
| **House of Fingolfin**, Noldorin dynasty | **Shield Marshal:** reorganizes threatened formations and preserves retreat. | **Pass-watch Fortress, building:** stone, timber and a supplied garrison secure approaches and relay warnings. | **Mounted Wardens, unit:** trained riders screen withdrawing infantry and observe exposed routes. |
| **House of Finarfin**, Noldorin dynasty | **Lore Envoy:** converts understanding and promises into practical cooperation. | **Hall of Welcome, building:** housing, provisions and learned staff support visitors, specialists and refugees. | **Restorative Song, prepared magic:** trained singers and rehearsal prepare bounded recovery; casualties are not resurrected. |
| **Falmari**, Aman Teleri | **Tide Captain:** coordinates demanding voyages and evacuations. | **Swan-ship, vessel:** seasoned timber, sailcloth and shipwright labor create efficient cargo and troop transport. | **Harbor Beacon, building:** stone, fuel and maintenance improve coastal approach safety and warning. |
| **Sindar**, Telerin people of Beleriand | **March Warden:** establishes concealed defenses and conditional passage. | **Hidden Watch-house, building:** timber, camouflage and observers produce discreet warning. | **Concealing Mantle, magic item:** specialist textile craft improves concealment in suitable cover. |
| **Nandor**, Telerin migration branch | **Woodland Pathfinder:** connects scattered communities through unobtrusive routes. | **Canopy Scouts, unit:** local trainees, bows and supplies provide reconnaissance and ambush. | **Forest Waystation, building:** modest timber and household labor support convoys without broad clearances. |
| **Avari authored clan**, independent Elven community | **Frontier Mediator:** connects settlements through limited, revisable agreements. | **Portable Workshop, support unit:** carts, tools and craftspeople maintain equipment during migration. | **Waymark Network, infrastructure:** surveys and local materials establish community-known routes and rendezvous points. |

## Exactly three human kingdoms

Each kingdom has **one hero type**, not three choices. Fortification, cavalry and maritime reach create different production priorities and different reasons to negotiate with neighbors.

| Kingdom | One unique hero type | Signature production 1 | Signature production 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gondor** | **Citadel Engineer:** coordinates fortification, repairs and organized withdrawal. | **Fortified Supply Depot, building:** stone, timber, preserved food and a garrison create a protected distribution point that extends operational endurance. | **Shield Company, unit:** metal, provisions and training produce infantry strongest on prepared ground and when covering engineers. |
| **Rohan** | **Rider Marshal:** assembles scattered companies for timely relief or flanking. | **Horse-breeding Stead, building:** pasture, fodder, handlers and time increase trained-mount supply; damaged grazing directly constrains output. | **Rider Company, unit:** mounts, arms and training create mobile regional forces dependent on suitable ground and continuing fodder. |
| **Númenor** | **Ocean Warden:** coordinates fleet logistics and long-distance coastal operations. | **Deep-water Shipyard, building:** timber, metal, rope, skilled workers and harbor access enable large-hull construction and repair. | **Expedition Ship, vessel:** shipyard work and outfitting produce transport and escort capacity; troops and stores compete for space. |

## Dwarven and Orc profiles

The Dwarven choices are cities and craft traditions, not three newly asserted ancestral houses. Shared ordinary mines and forges support different specialist outputs.

| Profile | One unique hero type | Signature production 1 | Signature production 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Khazad-dûm** | **Deep Surveyor:** finds valuable lodes and routes while managing excavation risk. | **Deepworks, building:** excavation, supports and tools open underground resources and passages. | **Tunnel Guard, unit:** armor, provisions and training equip defenders for confined approaches. |
| **Belegost** | **Armor Master:** adapts protection to a chosen threat. | **Mail Workshop, building:** metal, tools and patient labor produce durable armor and repairs. | **Masked Vanguard, unit:** specialized armor, protective masks and training help resist exceptional battlefield hazards. |
| **Nogrod** | **Master Artificer:** makes specialized tools with explicit material limits. | **Precision Forge, building:** selected ore, fuel and master tools support difficult commissions. | **Breach-tool Set, item:** exceptional steel equips a company to dismantle obstacles; use causes wear requiring repair. |
| **Orc fortress-clan** | **Warband Organizer:** coordinates rival companies and workshops through a chosen command doctrine. | **Salvage Foundry, building:** recovered equipment, fuel and labor restore metal and practical arms. | **Tunnel Raiders, unit:** tools, provisions and training support concealed approaches and attacks on exposed supply. |

Distinctive production should change decisions: ships require usable coasts, horses require fodder, concealed infrastructure requires cover, and specialist items require suitable bearers. Allies can exchange outputs while retaining their own production knowledge and single hero. Powerful hero abilities cannot replace these material dependencies.
