# Divine production rosters — revision 3

**All production, crafting, death and return rules are game inventions**, expressing character themes rather than reproducing Tolkien's metaphysics.

Each faction has **exactly one hero slot**, filled by crafting its named hero. Death empties it; rebuilding pays the full recipe and time again. No duplicate or additional heroes; ordinary units never promote into heroes. Equipment must be recovered or separately replaced.

**Stocks:** P provisions, M materials, K knowledge, E essence. Materials require timber/metal/stone biome tags. Essence comes from rare, contestable nodes, never worship. Units consume P; buildings/items chiefly require M; magic requires K/E. Manifestations and effigies are manufactured game entities, not additional heroes.

**Hero recipes:** each Vala and Melkor: **120M + 100K + 120E / four turns**. Sauron: **80M + 80K + 70E / three turns**. Pay upfront; unfinished heroes cannot act.

**Divine asymmetry:** extremely powerful heroes, two specialized building families, slow settlement growth, limited ordinary production. Shared housing/storehouses remain available; broad expansion needs trade. Even Aulë and Yavanna excel only within specialties. Sauron has stronger industry; Melkor militarizes production while exhausting its foundations.

Magic develops through research and hero use. Items are repeatable recipes unless later made unique; the binding ring is **not the One Ring**. Creatures require inputs and time. Vulnerable production, scarce essence and rebuilding delays constrain otherwise overwhelming divine heroes.
