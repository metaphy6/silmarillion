# ADR-0002: Static browser runtime and deterministic weekly orders

Status: accepted for implementation by the 2026-09-29 user request. Gameplay authority remains revision 6.

Phaser + TypeScript + Vite renders a pure TypeScript simulation. DOM controls own accessible reading and confirmation. IndexedDB owns transactional checkpoints. Network adapters carry commands and recipient-filtered projections; no renderer, DOM, wall clock or transport is imported by simulation.

Weekly plans reserve resources and 3 strategic operations plus 1 hero commitment against a player-local preview. Submission locks each seat. At resolution, command classes resolve in explicit phases (economy/queues, movement, other commitments, ordinary combat, prepared warnings, declared response movement, then weekly upkeep/production and objectives when tactical responses end), with initiative rotating by week then seat and per-seat sequence. Network arrival never orders simulation. Revalidation can reject a plan changed by opponents; rejected execution costs nothing. Every acceptance has a persistent command ID/sequence. The host is trusted and can cheat; hashes only detect divergence.

The default **Cross-era sandbox** is an invented basin of connected terrain analogues, not a historical map. All 55 profiles are available but duplicate factions/named identities, including both Melkor doctrines, are forbidden. Canonical unique artifacts (One Ring, Silmarils, named ships) are absent; original generic equipment has explicit item custody. No fixed canonical events. Routes between terrain analogues are invented and disclosed.

Provisional values outside r6 will live in typed content with an explicit status and a readable tuning ledger. They may fill unspecified ordinary prices, ranges measured on this board, health, damage and progression thresholds, but may not replace the semantic effects of adopted hero powers. The fifty older kits retain their current support-heavy design.

No PWA in v1: a pinned content/rules/protocol signature rejects mismatched saves and peers. An active browser session continues its loaded bundle; no automatic reload during a match. Host loss stops play; load the last committed authoritative checkpoint and reauthorize returning peers. Guest exports never contain authoritative hidden state.

Provider choice and service verification are documented separately after checking official APIs. Missing configuration leaves local play operational. An external private issuer requirement is a disclosed dependency, never a concealed owner backend.

## Integrated expansion boundaries

Physical convoys escrow existing P/M/K/E stock, load at staffed endpoints, move an existing ordinary carrier along a stored route and unload once. Loss preserves physical cargo; recovery and rerouting require explicit validated commands. Manwë replaces the ordinary relay requirement and spends readiness plus the personal commitment; it does not teleport cargo or waive travel/upkeep. Faction-wide stock remains the accounting model: separate settlement inventories are not implemented.

Repair queues modify existing compatible damaged equipment, constructs or fortifications. Materials, source access, staff, location and costs remain required; acceleration does not duplicate items. Durability and routine wear use disclosed provisional values. Terrain zones are revision-bounded simulation records, with physical clearing and movement/ranged consequences; renderer geometry is cosmetic and never advances them.

Diplomatic maps store offers. Only matching mutual peace/alliance creates an effective treaty; absent or mismatched consent is war, and either party may revoke consent. Guests receive other factions' offers toward their own seat, without third-party diplomatic maps. Convoy plans/cargo and repair queues remain owner-private; observed physical zones remain visible. These expansion decisions have regression coverage, not complete roster or balance validation.


## Physical substrate additions

Authored prey, mature vegetation, old woodland trails and shallow fords are scenario data, not resources generated by hero powers. Civilian households preserve finite population and local Provisions separately from faction stock; ordinary deposit/withdrawal debits the corresponding ledger exactly once. Household ledgers are not a fifth stock. Relocation requires actual carriers, open routes, capacity and explicit foreign refuge consent; capture invalidates that consent before committed checkpoints.

River-current authorization permits one physical bank-to-bank segment within the existing movement budget. The transport processor rechecks live movement penalties before entering the span; insufficient allowance pauses at the near bank rather than stranding the carrier or extending the authorization. Ordinary marine unloading creates one provisional cohesion step; Coastal Landing consumes its bound preparation to prevent only that new step.

Prepared ranged orders reserve one ordinary operation and resolve after an intervening response phase through the ordinary attack validator. They are distinct from immediate ordinary attacks and melee pursuit. Identified preparations aimed at a guest's own or allied targets have a minimal observation DTO; host future orders, paths and hidden identities remain private. Temporary living defenses lose active barrier metadata immediately when destroyed, while structural wreck identity remains for ordinary inspection.
