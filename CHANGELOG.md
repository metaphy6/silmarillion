# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- The Two Fords: a local real-time Gondor versus Saruman skirmish with delivered resources, worker construction, paid queues, raids, siege ammunition, hero commitments and stronghold victory.
- Direct battlefield selection/orders, formations, control groups, placement and rally points, actionable minimap, configurable hotkeys, scalable contextual HUD and separate versioned RTS saves.
- Original painted siege and forest atlases, directional workers and armies, construction stages, impacts and fog of war. The other 53 profiles and RTS multiplayer remain future work; the full weekly game is retained.
- Authored basin geography shared by traversal and original terrain paint, plus 88 directional figure paintings with articulated walk/work/attack motion and reduced-motion support.
- Locally bundled, licensed Noto typography with roster diacritics and large-text checks.
- Vairë's preserved production plans and Avari's conserved portable workshops, with physical loss, recovery, consent and normal paid queues.
- Local water/plant/track inspections, finite logging, routine worksite and habitat wear, tunnel warnings, paid reward agreements and carried reserve provisions.
- Physical beacon warnings, ordinary agent captivity, Forge repair kits, heavy equipment carriage, rescue preparation and care evacuation.
- Named faction companions, secondary production branches, military equipment and physical utility roles through ordinary paid production.
- Nienna's Council of Repair: recorded injuries, versioned consent, physical stock restitution, interrupted-cargo recovery and paid settlement under Economy.
- Private dated recovery records for known separated survivors after nearby retreats.

### Changed
- New matches use simulation version 13. Version 12 and earlier checkpoints and peers are incompatible; preserve original exports and use their original build. No save migration is provided.

### Fixed
- Positively identified software GPUs use Canvas rendering to avoid measured software WebGL framebuffer readback; hardware and unknown GPUs retain automatic selection.
- Keyboard confirmation, view changes and landscape toggling restore focus to a live control after rendering.
- Carried equipment can be fitted after travel using its actual bearer location without duplicating inventory or bonuses.
- Private guest checkpoints preserve visible mounted formations without exposing hidden mount lots; vessel contacts use their actual hull maximum.
- Stranded workshop escrow remains valid after its former carrier changes loads; invalid carrier orders reject safely.
- Paid ordinary rest recovers attached mount fatigue, and deployed ordinary devices cannot trigger another hero’s threshold passive.
