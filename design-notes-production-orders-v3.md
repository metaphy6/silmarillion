# Production factions: five Wizard orders and creature communities

**All five Istar orders and these production rules are original game inventions**, replacing named-Wizard selection. Creature institutions and magical production are freely adapted.

**Common contract:** exactly one fixed hero type per faction, at most one living hero. Generate appearance/personality, not a different class. Create and replace dead heroes using their listed building, recipe and production time. Other units are ordinary nonheroes. Numerical costs follow the shared economy; each faction also builds housing, storage and economic infrastructure.

## Five original Istar orders

| Faction and identity | Single hero; creation building and recipe | Two signature unit products | Crafted item and strategic distinction |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ember Order:** courage, shelter and collective resolve | **Kindler**, created at the Hearth Sanctuary with provisions, worked silver and an ember focus | Hearth Wardens: recruited defensive companies. Ashlights: summoned spirits that sustain an occupied refuge | **Oath Lantern**, forged from silver, resin and an ember focus; regroups allies. Shelters make collective resilience its strength. |
| **Grove Order:** cultivation, symbiosis and recovery | **Grovekeeper**, created at the Seedwell with herbs, living wood and a growth focus | Thornkeepers: recruited garden defenders. Moth Clouds: summoned reconnaissance and concealment swarms | **Renewal Charm**, woven from herbs, fiber and a growth focus; supports treatment. Nurseries produce defenders and medicines. |
| **Veil Order:** intelligence, passage and misdirection | **Veilweaver**, created at the Hidden Observatory with cloth, glass and a mist focus | Quiet Envoys: recruited infiltrators and negotiators. Mist Doubles: summoned decoys that cannot transport goods | **False-Signal Seal**, made from glass, ink and a mist focus; creates a misleading report. Safehouses enable infiltration. |
| **Forge Order:** construction, maintenance and distributed industry | **Artificer**, created at the Arcane Foundry with tools, alloy and a binding focus | Wrought Sentinels: manufactured defensive constructs. Bound Sparks: summoned workshop assistants | **Repair Matrix**, built from alloy, tools and a binding focus; repairs supplied equipment. Service depots sustain industrial networks. |
| **Star Order:** navigation, warning and coordinated intervention | **Wayseer**, created at the Astral Hall with crystal, charts and a star focus | Beacon Riders: recruited couriers and scouts. Star Motes: summoned watchers along prepared routes | **Wayglass**, ground from crystal and charged with a star focus; reads beacon messages. Observatories coordinate distant allies. |

Orders manufacture their own focus from local materials. Focus types are recipe categories, not universal resource bars. Summons occupy binding capacity; permanent units require maintenance. More workshops increase production, never the hero limit.

## Creature and small-community factions

| Faction | Single hero; creation building and recipe | Two signature products and required buildings | Magical item production |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ent Grove** | **Grove Elder**; Moot Grove; water, stored vitality, heartwood | Sapling Guardians at a Living Nursery; Root Shelters grown by a Rootworks | **Rootward Totem**, heartwood and cultivated sap: anchors local protection. |
| **Eagle Eyrie** | **Skywarden**; High Nest; provisions, nestwork, sky essence | Scout Flights at a Training Ledge; Rescue Flights at a Harness Perch | **Wind Knot**, feathers, cord and sky essence: steadies one loaded flight. |
| **Dragon Dominion** | **Hoard Sovereign**; Crown Lair; provisions, treasure, furnace essence | Lesser Drakes at a Brood Vault; Cinderlings at an Ember Fissure | **Hoard Brand**, metal, treasure and furnace essence: wards a selected cache. |
| **Troll Hold** | **Stonebreaker**; Chieftain's Hall; provisions, stone, iron | Shield Trolls at a Drill Yard; Siege Crews at a Breach Workshop | **Stonehide Plate**, stone, iron and purchased enchantment: protects a vulnerable approach. |
| **Wolf Pack** | **Packwarden**; Gathering Den; provisions, scent resin, moon essence | Trackers at a Scent Den; Runners at a Pack Ground | **Moonfang Token**, bone, resin and moon essence: strengthens coordinated pursuit. |
| **Spider Brood** | **Brood Matriarch**; Royal Hollow; prey, silk, venom essence | Weblayers at a Silk Nursery; Venom Stalkers at a Brood Chamber | **Nightweb Snare**, silk and venom essence: conceals a prepared trap. |
| **Hobbit Shire** | **Shirekeeper**; Common Hall; provisions, tools, shared treasury | Fieldhands at a Commons Farm; Bounders at a Watch House | **Hearthward Lantern**, glass, oil and purchased enchantment: guards a shelter against panic. |
| **Optional Balrog Host** | **Flame Tyrant**; Black Crucible; iron, fuel, dread essence | Cinder Guards at a War Barracks; Furnace Wights at a Binding Pit | **Searing Standard**, iron, cloth and dread essence: supports a declared assault. Neither produced unit is another Balrog hero. |

Creature production uses disclosed magical or recruitment schedules, allowing game-ready units within campaign time. Items require materials and crafting time. Enchantments come from trade or a costly generic enchanting workshop, keeping isolated factions playable.
